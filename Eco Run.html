Garbage Run

Pick up litter, grab coins, and carry each item to its correct bin inside the safe zone. Pollution monsters can’t follow you in there. A wrong bin or a monster hit costs health.

Hold an arrow key or WASD to move, and release to stop. On touch, drag on the maze or hold the pad. Press M or tap 🔊 to mute.
